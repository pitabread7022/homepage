I like understanding how things work, then making them work better.

I did Physics and Aerospace Engineering at IIT Kanpur, which mostly taught me one thing: write down the physics first, then let the AI fill in what the equations can’t. That’s still how I work.

These days I spend my time on solar plants. I built the monitoring backbone that watches dozens of sites live, the simulations that tell us how a plant should be performing, and the 3D and VR twins that let you walk around one without leaving your desk.

Outside of work I fall down rabbit holes about relativity, information theory and where AGI might be heading. Agra is home, Gurgaon is where I build, and I’m open to moving to the US or EU for the right adventure.

Selected work, told as problems and results.

Solar SCADA, live

24 sites · 43,200 data points per second

Problem
Operators need to see how every plant is really performing against how it should, in real time.
What I built
IoT ingestion, PVLIB and PyBaMM “ideal plant” baselines, health models per data stream, QR-based datalogger mapping, and a RAG chatbot for KPI questions. Deployed on Kubernetes with CI/CD.
Result
Live monitoring of 24 sites, with actual versus expected performance side by side.

Rooftops from space

30 cm stereo imagery · 1,776 buildings

Problem
Small rooftop objects like HVAC units and tanks decide what fits on a roof, and a flat photo can’t show their height.
What I built
A 3D small-object detection pipeline on stereo satellite pairs, a labelled GIS dataset, and a self-hosted multimodal agentic LLM system on an NVIDIA DGX Spark.
Result
90% functional accuracy on building footprints and heights.

Twins you can walk through

PINNs · Unity · Meta Quest · Three.js

Problem
Asset health is hard to see, and plants are hard to visit.
What I built
A PINN-based inverter twin for predictive maintenance, a Unity VR twin with live dashboards and AI chat, and a U-Net and SAM pipeline that turns drone imagery into 3D plant layouts.
Result
A physics-informed twin plus a walkable VR plant, fed by live data.
